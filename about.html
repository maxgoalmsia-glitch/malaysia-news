About Maxgoalasia 288

An independent sports news portal built for Malaysian fans — bringing you news, match updates and event highlights from the sports you love.

Maxgoalasia 288 is an independent Malaysian sports news portal. We publish sports news, match updates, previews, results and event highlights across football, basketball, badminton, motorsports and grassroots local sport. Our goal is simple: to be the clearest, fastest and most trustworthy place for Malaysian sports fans to stay informed.

We are not affiliated with any league, federation, club or governing body. Our editorial coverage is produced independently so that readers get straightforward reporting and honest context on the stories that matter.

What we cover

From the Super League and the national football team to badminton’s international circuit, domestic basketball, and motorsport weekends at Sepang, we follow the competitions Malaysians care about most. Alongside the headlines, we highlight the grassroots clubs and young athletes shaping the future of Malaysian sport.

Our values

Independent

No affiliations or hidden agendas — just clear, fan-first reporting.

Fast & accurate

Timely match updates and verified information you can rely on.

For the fans

Built to celebrate Malaysian sport at every level, everywhere.

Our story

Maxgoalasia 288 started as a small passion project among friends who wanted a better way to keep up with local and international sport from a Malaysian perspective. Today it has grown into a dedicated portal read by fans across the country. We remain committed to the same idea we began with — putting readers first.
