Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Last updated: 3 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Malaysia Sports Hub (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and protects the information you provide when you visit www.malaysiasportshub.com (the “Website”). By using the Website, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

We aim to collect only the information we need to operate the Website and respond to you. This may include:

  • Information you give us — such as your name and email address when you subscribe to our newsletter or submit our contact form.
  • Usage information — such as pages visited, browser type, device and approximate location, collected automatically to help us understand how the Website is used.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — small files stored on your device to remember preferences and measure traffic (see section 5).

2. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send our newsletter and respond to your enquiries;
  • Operate, maintain and improve the Website and its content;
  • Understand readership and measure the performance of our pages;
  • Protect the Website against fraud, abuse and security threats; and
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations.

3. Legal basis and your consent

Where required, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent (for example, newsletter sign-ups), to perform a service you request, or for our legitimate interests in operating a sports news portal. You may withdraw consent at any time.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers who help us operate the Website (such as email delivery or analytics providers), and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information where required by law.

5. Cookies

The Website may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and to gather anonymous statistics about traffic. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the Website.

6. Third-party links

Our pages may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites and encourage you to review their policies.

7. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children’s privacy

The Website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, update or request deletion of your personal information, and to unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

12. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@malaysiasportshub.com or through our contact page.
